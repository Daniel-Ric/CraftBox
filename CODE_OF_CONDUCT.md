# Code of Conduct

CraftBox welcomes bug reports, questions, technical disagreement and contributions. Everyone taking part should be able to do so without harassment or personal attacks.

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other communication spaces managed for CraftBox. It also applies when someone represents the project in a public space.

## Working together

- Discuss the code, evidence and proposed behavior. Keep criticism about the work rather than the person.
- Give people room to ask questions, correct mistakes and learn unfamiliar parts of the project.
- Explain disagreements clearly. Reproduction steps and concrete examples are more useful than repeated demands.
- Respect other people's time. Avoid duplicate reports, unsolicited mentions and pressure for immediate replies.
- Accept that a contribution may need changes or may fall outside the project's scope. Maintainers are responsible for what the project accepts.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment and personal attacks are not acceptable. Neither are impersonation, deliberate disruption, spam or repeated attempts to continue a conversation after moderation has ended it.

Do not publish another person's private information, account credentials, private messages or identifying capture data without permission. Do not submit malicious code or use project spaces to organize attacks against players, servers or services.

## Captures and third-party content

CraftBox works with worlds, skins, resource packs and network recordings. A useful bug report does not need to expose another player's information or redistribute an entire server pack.

Share only content you have permission to share. Remove credentials and unnecessary personal data from logs, screenshots, captures and sample projects. Credit original authors, including bedrocktool contributors and the creators of third-party assets.

For a suspected vulnerability or leaked credential, use the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting misconduct

Contact the maintainer, **SpindexGFX**, privately on [Discord](https://discord.com/users/252138923952832523). Include links to the relevant conversation, a short account of what happened and any context needed to understand it. Share sensitive evidence privately rather than posting it in an issue.

If private contact is unavailable, a brief request for maintainer contact can be posted in the repository without names, allegations or sensitive evidence. Do not publish a detailed conduct report there.

Reports will be handled with care. Information may need to be shared with people involved in moderation; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Moderation

Maintainers may ask for a change in behavior, remove content, lock a conversation or restrict participation. Serious or repeated misconduct may lead to a permanent restriction. The response should reflect the behavior, its impact and any relevant previous incidents.

Good-faith disagreement, reporting a bug or declining a contribution is not by itself misconduct. Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report is not acceptable.
